BARRIER FIX

Changes:
1. Higher proposal contract type changed from CALL to HIGHER.
2. Lower proposal contract type changed from PUT to LOWER.
3. One positive offset field is used.
4. Higher barrier is automatically +offset.
5. Lower barrier is automatically -offset.
6. UI shows the barriers that will be sent.

Example:
Offset barrier distance = 0.5
Higher request barrier = +0.5
Lower request barrier  = -0.5

After uploading the corrected public/index.html and public/service-worker.js to GitHub,
Render should auto-deploy the new commit.
