Hedge settings

Only Ups/Only Downs (RUNHIGH/RUNLOW) has no barrier and is always priced in ticks — duration unit is locked to Ticks and the barrier fields are hidden while this mode is selected. Deriv restricts the allowed tick count for these contracts (commonly a small range like 2–10); if the duration you enter is out of range, the exact Deriv error will show in the Activity log below.

Higher and Lower are separate Deriv orders sent as close together as possible. Exact same-tick execution is not guaranteed and hedging does not guarantee profit.
